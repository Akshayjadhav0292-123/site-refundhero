Frequently asked questions

Is RefundHero free?

This starter version is free to use. Hosting or extra services may have their own limits or charges, so check the terms before adding paid features.

Does this website send the email for me?

No. It creates a draft in your browser. You review it and send it yourself.

Will I definitely get a refund?

No outcome can be guaranteed. Your options depend on the seller's policy, the facts and applicable local rules.

Are my details saved?

This starter version does not send your form details to a server or save them to an account. Avoid entering sensitive personal or payment information.
